Simple hanger — Isaac Sim asset

Load assembly1_white_plastic_cyl_collision_static.usda for fixed scenery or
assembly1_white_plastic_cyl_collision_dynamic.usda for a 0.25 kg rigid body.
Keep the asset files together.

The team-created OBJ uses meters, a centered XY origin, and bottom plane Z=0.
The material is off-white rough plastic. Convex cylinder colliders cover:
- Base: radius 80 mm, height 15 mm, vertical Z.
- Pole: radius 11 mm, Z=15–185 mm.
- Branch: radius 6 mm, length 110.19 mm, 20 degrees above horizontal toward -Y;
  root [0, -9.21955, 168.3561] mm, tip [0, -112.76295, 206.0421] mm.

Inspect preview_cyl_collision.png and collision_proxy_stl/ for collider geometry.
