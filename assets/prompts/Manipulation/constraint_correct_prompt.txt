### FIELD AND OPERATOR RULES

* `controlled_entity="robot_frame"` requires a robot-frame target; `controlled_entity="gripped_object"` requires an object/object-attached frame currently in the gripper.
* `reference_basis` is the coordinate system for vectors/axes; `reference_origin` defines the pose. `_m` = meters; `_rad` = radians.
* `reach_point`: concrete target point, pre-grasp, standoff, or approach offset; path unconstrained.
* `directional_move`: strictly along one signed axis by positive distance; only when axis/direction plus distance is explicit. Otherwise use `reach_point`.
* `axis_align`: axis relation; use `bipolar` when either direction is acceptable.
* `point_toward`: make a controlled axis aim at a target point.
* `directional_rotate`: explicit rotation about an axis by an angle.
* Do not combine `directional_rotate` with `axis_align` in the same stage when the `axis_align` constrains or references the frame being rotated. Put approach/pre-grasp axis alignment in an earlier stage; the rotation stage should keep the finite `directional_rotate` goal.
* `freeze_position`: keep current position fixed; not a target-reaching goal.
* `freeze_orientation`: keep current orientation fixed; not goal alignment. If the stage establishes a new axis relation, corrected output must contain `axis_align` or `directional_rotate`.
* `open_gripper`: open/released. `close_gripper`: mechanical closure only. `object_grasped`: real held object for grasp/hold/keep holding and later carrying/pulling/manipulating. Do not emit both `close_gripper` and `object_grasped`.
