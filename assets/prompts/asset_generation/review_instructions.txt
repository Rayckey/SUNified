Review a candidate simulation asset against the original video frames
and the user's description. The images named frame_* are observations. preview.png is six labeled
canonical renders of the candidate; collisions.png shows its compound collision geometry.
Viewpoints are not calibrated to the video: compare shape and proportions across views, not pixel alignment.
Prioritize silhouette, relative proportions, missing or extra parts, grasp/hang openings, cup interiors,
contact surfaces, and coarse color. Check which parts should move together.
For drawers, require separate moving bodies with real hollow boxes and handles attached to those bodies,
prismatic joints pointing out of the cabinet, sensible limits, and cabinet clearances. Check the open-pose
preview (last image when articulated): all drawers and handles must extend in the correct direction,
with open interiors and no obvious penetration of stationary shelves or partitions. This preview is
kinematic, not a physics test. A cabinet flattened into one body is unacceptable. Multiple clips may
show removed parts: use the description to check the final assembled configuration and drawer count.
Read metadata for dimensions and scale provenance. Distinguish real mismatches from viewpoint/lighting.
Check semantic descriptions and interaction frames in the candidate and metadata: handles and cavity
centers must be on the correct moving body, hinge frames at hinges, and local axes consistent with
geometry. Require useful interaction points for every moving joint; flag misplaced or invented points.
The score is subjective visual fidelity, NOT geometric accuracy or a measured confidence interval.
Set acceptable only if no major visible or functional mismatch remains. Provide specific geometric changes
for the builder. Unseen surfaces, camera calibration, material parameters, physical stability and unmeasured
dimensions remain uncertain even at a high score. Do not claim a physics simulation has been run.
